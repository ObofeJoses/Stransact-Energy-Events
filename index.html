Executive workshop: Energy in Africa, Nairobi 2026

The webinar raises the questions. The workshop works through them: three days of teaching and case work for senior people in the energy value chain, framed by a welcome cocktail dinner and a day in Nairobi National Park. Monday 30 November to Friday 4 December 2026.

Who should attend

  • Chief financial officers and finance directors of energy companies
  • Tax, legal and commercial leads
  • Utility and distribution company executives
  • Regulators and public officials
  • Investors, lenders and project developers

What you take away

  • A working grasp of the fiscal and regulatory regimes that shape returns
  • A method to value, structure and finance an energy transaction
  • Practical answers on metering, losses and distribution economics
  • An action plan for your organisation, tested with faculty

How it runs

  • Three teaching modules that mirror the webinar's themes
  • A team deal lab that runs through the three teaching days
  • One-to-one clinics with Stransact partners and directors, by appointment
  • Guest faculty from operators, regulators and financiers, to be announced

Programme outline

The three modules follow the webinar's three segments, in the same order. Each opens with the questions webinar registrants submitted, including those the panel could not reach. Guest faculty will be confirmed as registration opens.

Monday 30 NovemberArrival

Arrival and cocktail dinner

  • Airport transfers and hotel check-in
  • Registration desk opens at 2:00 p.m.
  • Welcome cocktail dinner, 7:00 p.m.
  • No formal session
Tuesday 1 DecemberModule 1

Oil, gas and the transition

  • From the webinar, then the sector overview. The value chain, the PIA 2021, Electricity Act 2023 and Nigeria Tax Act 2025, the IOC divestments, and the investment outlook
  • Market entry. Entry routes by segment, business vehicles, licences and permits, local content
  • The upstream sector. The PIA and its Amendment Bill, NUPRC and NCDMB, production sharing contracts, joint ventures and marginal fields, domestic gas and flaring, host communities, LNG
  • Midstream and downstream. NMDPRA, pipelines and gas infrastructure, refining, LPG and CNG, distribution and trading licences
  • What travels. Nigeria as a model for African oil and gas development
  • Deal lab, part 1. Teams form, receive the transaction, and choose the entry route, vehicle and licences
Wednesday 2 DecemberModule 2

Power: regulation, distribution and metering

  • From the webinar, then gas-to-power. Gas-to-power contracting and gas supply agreements
  • The electricity market. The Electricity Act 2023 and state laws, NERC and state regulators, GenCos, TCN, DisCos, NBET and the emerging state markets
  • Generation, contracts and tariffs. Generation licensing, power purchase agreements, tariff regulation, embedded and captive power, independent distribution networks
  • Metering, losses and the regulator. Electricity theft, metering and collection risks for investors, financing the metering gap, licensing and enforcement
  • What travels. The East African experience in renewables and geothermal (guest faculty)
  • Deal lab, part 2. Teams settle the offtake, regulatory approvals and revenue risks
Thursday 3 DecemberModule 3

Tax, fiscal terms and capital

  • From the webinar, then the fiscal regime. The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the fiscal regime by segment: upstream, gas, midstream and downstream, electricity
  • Transaction taxes, compliance and controversy. VAT, withholding tax, customs and stamp duties; permanent establishment risk; filing, reporting and IFRS; audits and dealing with the revenue authority
  • Investment and capital. JV and farm-in structures, project finance and DFI funding, foreign exchange and repatriation, transfer pricing, exit and divestment, dispute resolution and arbitration
  • What travels. Structuring cross-border energy investment within Africa
  • Deal lab presentations and close. Teams finance the transaction, price its tax cost, plan the exit and present to a faculty panel; action plans and certificates of attendance
Friday 4 DecemberExcursion and closing dinner

Nairobi National Park and Carnivore

  • Closing dinner at Carnivore, Nairobi
  • Departures from Saturday 5 December
